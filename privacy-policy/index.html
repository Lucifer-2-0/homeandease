WhatsApp enquiries and staff applications

The contact page opens WhatsApp with a prepared message to +234 708 600 7268. The visitor decides whether to send it. WhatsApp processes any message after it is sent under its own terms and privacy policy; do not share a full home address until the business confirms a suitable way to exchange it.

This implementation does not add analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential cookies. The owner should review whether those external resources and any future hosting tools require additional disclosure or configuration.

Questions, retention and contact

The WhatsApp number is +234 708 600 7268. The legal data controller, application data handling, retention period and privacy contact still require owner confirmation. The owner must provide these details and approve the final notice before launch.
