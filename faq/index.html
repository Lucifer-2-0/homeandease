Client FAQs

Client FAQStaff FAQ
How do I get started?

Send us a WhatsApp message at +234 708 600 7268. We’ll ask a few questions to understand your needs before recommending a service.

When do you share pricing?

After we understand your needs and explain how the service works. Pricing always comes last. However, you can view our price list on our website or our WhatsApp Catalogue.

How long does matching take?

Matching takes up to 48-72 hours after confirmation and payment. If there is a delay, we will always communicate ahead of time.

Are your staff screened?

Yes. All staff are carefully screened and assessed before being assigned to any home. We also carry out medical and legal checks before hiring and adding them to our staff pool.

What if I’m not comfortable with the match?

We take concerns seriously and address them promptly. Our staff replacement policy is shared with all clients before any agreement or payment is made. Where applicable, staff may be replaced and fees refunded in line with this policy.

Is this a monthly service?

Yes. Our services are offered on a monthly subscription, which we explain clearly before you confirm. You only pay for what you need, when you need it—and you’re free to cancel at any time.

When does service start?

Once matching is complete, we introduce the staff member and confirm your start date.
