Work With Us in a safe and supportive home care team

We believe good care starts with people who feel respected, protected, and supported. If you are a nanny, maid, or cleaner looking for fair pay, clear duties, and a healthy place to work, we welcome you.

Home Assistants (Maids)

Our home assistants help keep your home clean, organised, and running smoothly day to day. They focus on housekeeping tasks like cleaning, laundry, kitchen support, and basic organisation, so your living space stays calm and functional. Their role is centered on maintaining the home—not childcare—so expectations stay clear and everyone is comfortable.

Nannies

Our nannies are trained to focus fully on your children’s care, safety, and daily routines. From feeding and hygiene to play, learning, and supervision, their attention stays on supporting your child’s growth and wellbeing. They handle child-related tidying only, allowing parents to feel confident that childcare is thoughtful, structured, and attentive.

Professional Cleaners

Our professional cleaners are deep-cleaning specialists. They come in on scheduled sessions to thoroughly clean and refresh your space—bathrooms, kitchens, floors, and hard-to-reach areas. This service is ideal for periodic resets, and helps keep your home feeling fresh, healthy, and well cared for.

How much will you earn?

Our staff earn over ₦70,000 - ₦100,000 per month.

Clear work duties

We make sure your job is clear from the start.

  • A nanny is hired only to care for children.
  • A maid or cleaner is hired only for home cleaning and related duties.

Clients are informed of this clearly, so you are not asked to do work outside your role.

Work hours

Normal work hours are 7:00am to 5:00pm. If a client asks for extra hours, those hours are paid separately. We encourage rest and family time, especially on weekends

  • Nannies And Home Assistants: 4 - 5 days a week OR live with staff.
  • Cleaners: 1- 5 days a week (time dependent on client)

Other benefits

When you work with us, you may also receive:

  • Transport allowance when needed
  • Training to help you learn basic digital skills
  • Opportunities for personal and skill development
  • Support from a team that listens and responds to your concerns

We care about your wellbeing

Your safety and wellbeing matter to us. We work hard to protect our staff and keep a respectful and professional work environment. You will be treated with dignity, fairness, and care.

If you want to work in a place where your role is respected and your effort is valued, we encourage you to apply and learn more.
